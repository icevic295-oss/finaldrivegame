How to play

Every day you take over one real NFL final drive — same drive for everyone, straight from the play-by-play record. Move the ball, manage the clock, score to tie or take the lead.

The rules

  • The clock is the real opponent. Incomplete passes, out-of-bounds catches, spikes and timeouts stop it. Everything else keeps it running.
  • You call every snap: pass, run, field goal, spike, or a timeout.
  • The drive dies on a turnover, a turnover on downs, a missed field goal — or when the clock hits zero.
  • Win the drive by driving your team to a tie or the lead.

Every call is graded

Each call is compared against win probability from real NFL data: how much did this call move your chances, versus what the best available call was worth? You'll see green (great call), yellow (fine), orange (shaky) or red (that cost you the game).

Calls that match what really happened replay the real outcome. Off-script calls resolve with league-average results for that situation — so yes, "off script" usually means "worse".

The scoring

  • 🟢 great call — gained more win probability than expected
  • 🟡 fine — about what the situation called for
  • 🟠 shaky — left value on the table
  • 🔴 costly — the win probability chart went the wrong way

Then argue about it

When the drive ends you get your grade, your coaching style, and a comparison against what the real coach actually did. Copy your results and defend them. That's the whole point.
